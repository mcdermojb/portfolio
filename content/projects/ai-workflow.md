### Objective: Scaling Content Efficiency
In rapid release environments, the Documentation Development Life Cycle (DDLC) often faces bottlenecks. I reduced content cycle times by 30-40% without compromising the technical accuracy required for enterprise security products.

### Execution: Engineering the Pipeline
I integrated Large Language Models (LLMs) as architectural tools to automate content management:

* **Automated API Drafting:** Engineered prompt templates to ingest Swagger/OpenAPI specifications, generating technically grounded initial drafts from source code.
* **Large-Scale Auditing:** Orchestrated a batch-processing workflow for **3,000 legacy topics**, using LLMs to identify content issues: process flows, ontologies, or redundant and outdated content.
* **Style Governance:** Created prompt-based QA templates to enforce brand voice and terminology.

### Results
* **Cycle Reduction:** Decreased content creation and review time by **~40%**.
* **Shift to Strategy:** Transitioned the writer’s role from draft generator to subject matter expert and editor.
* **Operational Scale:** Audited massive legacy repositories in days rather than months.
