### Challenge: Resolving Workflow Bottlenecks
Managing documentation for N-tier architectures requires a robust delivery pipeline. The legacy workflow was unable to scale with rapid software release cycles and in particular for maintaining multiple releases at scale.

### Strategy: Systems Implementation
I led the transition to a modern structured authoring environment:

* **Comparative Analysis:** Conducted workflow mapping and usability testing to evaluate **AEM vs. Vasont**, aligning the CCMS with global engineering requirements.
* **DITA Standardization:** Implemented DITA standards to enforce content reuse across diverse product lines.
* **Git Integration:** Integrated structured API models within Git to streamline the hand-off between engineering and documentation.

### Results
* **Publishing Speed:** Reduced total publishing time by **40%** and increased delivery speed by **25%**.
* **Support Reduction:** Decreased installation-related support calls by **90%** through improved content scaffolding.
* **Global Alignment:** Unified the content strategy for complex, multi-tier enterprise environments.
